# Huge slow — 20x20 mixed palette

> Same mixed palette on a 20x20 grid. Hypothesis: more room = longer wait for interactions. Does it feel epic or empty?

## Setup

- **Grid:** 20 × 20
- **Palette:** Block, Beehive, Eater, GliderSE
- **Advance steps per turn:** 4
- **Rules:** Same as baseline except 20x20

### Design questions this scenario tests

- Do gliders take too many turns to meet anything?
- Does the dish feel sparse when most of it is empty?
- Is 12 turns enough for a meaningful match on this grid?


---

## Legend

- `.` dead (empty agar)
- `X` Player 1
- `Y` Player 2
- `*` neutral (contested birth)

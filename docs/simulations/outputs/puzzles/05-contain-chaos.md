# Puzzle 05 — Contain the Chaos

> SOLO PUZZLE. An R-pentomino sits at the center. Left alone it will explode for hundreds of generations. Goal: place Eaters to contain the explosion so final alive count ≤ 12 after the simulation runs. Teaches that named tools can tame even the most chaotic seeds.

## Setup

- **Grid:** 14 × 14
- **Palette:** Eater
- **Advance steps per turn:** 4
- **Rules:** Solo mode. Prompt: 'Contain the specimen. Final alive count ≤ 12 cells. 2 moves.' Win check: final count.

### Design questions this scenario tests

- Is 2 eaters enough to contain an R-pent?
- Does the climactic-final-puzzle framing work?
- Is this too hard without trial and error?


---

## Legend

- `.` dead (empty agar)
- `X` Player 1
- `Y` Player 2
- `*` neutral (contested birth)

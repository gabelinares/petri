# Puzzle 05 — Silence at Range

> SOLO PUZZLE. A Blinker oscillates far across the dish. Goal: destroy it in 1 move using a Glider launched from the opposite corner. Teaches that named Conway tools combine: a glider (spaceship, known behavior) destroys a blinker (oscillator, known behavior) from across the map. Feels like a long-range strike — the ultimate demonstration of 'deploy don't predict'.

## Setup

- **Grid:** 12 × 12
- **Palette:** GliderSE
- **Advance steps per turn:** 4
- **Rules:** Solo mode. Prompt: 'Destroy the specimen from across the dish in 1 move.' Win check: 0 live cells at end (or very little debris, < 5).

### Design questions this scenario tests

- Does the 'long-range strike' feel land as a climactic puzzle?
- Is the glider's trajectory clear enough that the player can aim by eye?
- Are 4–5 advance presses okay, or does that feel like too much waiting?

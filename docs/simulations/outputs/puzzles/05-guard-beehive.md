# Puzzle 05 — Guard the Beehive

> SOLO PUZZLE. A Beehive (stable still life) sits in the center. A Glider is incoming from the NW and will destroy it. Goal: stop the glider in 1 move so the Beehive stays intact. Teaches defensive placement + that still lifes need protection from spaceships.

## Setup

- **Grid:** 12 × 12
- **Palette:** Block, Eater
- **Advance steps per turn:** 4
- **Rules:** Solo mode. Prompt: 'Keep the Beehive alive. Block the incoming glider in 1 move.' Win check: Beehive cells (X) still present at end, glider fully neutralized.

### Design questions this scenario tests

- Does 'stop a threat before it arrives' read as a strategic puzzle?
- Is the glider's trajectory visibly readable so the player can aim?
- Does ghost preview of 'beehive still alive after advance' feel like the right tell?
